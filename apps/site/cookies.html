1. What Are Cookies & Trackers?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, remember preferences, and provide information to site owners about how their site is being used.

Beyond cookies, similar technologies include tracking pixels (tiny invisible images that log a server request when a page loads), web beacons, embedded scripts, e-tags, local storage, and session storage. All of these are covered by this policy and referred to collectively as "Trackers."

There are two types of Trackers on this Website:

  • First-party Trackers — set directly by Clockwrk to operate the Website
  • Third-party Trackers — set by external services we use (Google, Meta, X, LinkedIn, Reddit, etc.) which may access and use the data they collect in accordance with their own privacy policies

2. Why We Use Trackers

Clockwrk uses Trackers for four purposes:

  • Essential operation — to keep the Website secure and functioning correctly
  • Analytics — to understand how visitors use the Website so we can improve it
  • Advertising & remarketing — to measure the effectiveness of our paid campaigns across Google, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and Reddit, and to show relevant Clockwrk ads to people who have previously visited the Website
  • Functional — to enable certain third-party content embedded in the Website (such as fonts)

We do not use Trackers to build profiles for selling to data brokers, to track you across third-party sites for non-advertising purposes, or for any purpose not described here.

3. Tracker Categories

Strictly Necessary Always Active

Help us understand how visitors find and use the Website — pages visited, session duration, traffic sources, device types. We use Google Analytics. Data is aggregated and IP addresses are anonymised. Disabling these does not affect core site functionality.

10. Consent & EU/UK Visitors

Under the EU ePrivacy Directive, the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), and the GDPR, we are required to obtain your consent before setting non-essential Trackers for visitors from the EU or UK.

When you first visit the Website from an EU or UK IP address, a cookie consent banner will be displayed. The current banner provides the following choices:

  • Accept all — enables analytics, advertising, social media, and functional Trackers
  • Deny all — disables all optional Trackers and allows only strictly necessary Trackers

Your preference is stored and respected on subsequent visits. If you want to change your choice, you can clear your browser cookies for this Website and make a new selection when the banner appears again, or contact us at hello@clockwrk.io. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Strictly necessary Trackers are always active regardless of consent.

12. Opting Out of Interest-Based Advertising

In addition to the per-platform controls above and your browser settings, you can opt out of interest-based advertising globally through the following industry opt-out tools:

  • Your Online Choices — EU & UK
  • Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out — US
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-Out — US. The DAA also offers the AppChoices mobile app for controlling interest-based advertising within mobile apps.
  • DAAC Opt-Out — Canada
  • DDAI Opt-Out — Japan

Note that opting out does not mean you will stop seeing Clockwrk ads entirely — it means those ads will no longer be personalised based on your browsing behaviour.

13. Consequences of Denying Trackers

Users are free to decide whether or not to allow the use of Trackers. However, please note that Trackers help this Website to provide a better experience and certain advanced functionalities. If the User chooses to block the use of Trackers, the Owner may be unable to provide related features, which may include:

  • Proper attribution of referral codes if referral session cookies are blocked
  • Accurate analytics data which the Owner uses to improve the Website
  • Conversion tracking that allows the Owner to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns

Blocking strictly necessary Trackers (such as Cloudflare security cookies) may cause the Website to malfunction or become inaccessible.

14. Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) signal that can be sent to websites to indicate a preference not to be tracked. There is currently no universal standard for how websites must respond to DNT signals, and not all third-party services honour them.

Where technically feasible, we respect DNT signals by not activating optional analytics and advertising Trackers for visitors who have sent a DNT signal. We cannot guarantee that all third-party services embedded in the Website will do the same — Users are encouraged to use the per-platform opt-out tools in Section 11 for the most reliable control.

EU and UK visitors are best protected by using the cookie consent mechanism described in Section 10, which allows them to accept all optional Trackers or deny all optional Trackers regardless of DNT support.
