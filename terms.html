Clockwrk provides a subscription-based model for ongoing design and development work, delivered on a per-request basis. The Service includes but is not limited to: UI/UX design, graphic design, branding and visual identity, frontend and backend web development, Webflow and Framer development, outdoor and print design, web presence assets, ERP systems, e-commerce, mobile app design, pitch decks, social media design, and email templates.

A "Request" is one clearly scoped, deliverable unit of work. A Request is not an entire project — complex projects are broken into sequential or parallel Requests depending on the User's plan tier. The Owner will clarify scope and confirm understanding before beginning work on any Request. Vague or incomplete briefs may delay the commencement of a Request.

Turnaround Time

The Owner targets delivery of each completed Request within 2–3 business days from the moment a Request is clearly defined, confirmed, and all necessary assets and information have been received from the User. Plans are structured as Startup (1 active Request at a time), Business (2 active Requests at a time), and Enterprise (3 active Requests at a time), each available on a weekly or monthly billing cycle.
