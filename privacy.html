6. Advertising & Marketing

Clockwrk runs paid advertising campaigns to promote its services on Google, Facebook, Instagram, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and Reddit. To measure the effectiveness of these campaigns and show relevant Clockwrk ads to people who have visited our Website, we use advertising pixels and tracking tags from each platform.

Meta Pixel (Facebook & Instagram)

The Meta Pixel tracks visitor actions on our Website and shares this data with Meta Platforms Inc. to measure ad conversions, build remarketing audiences of past visitors, and build lookalike audiences. Meta acts as a joint controller for this data. You can manage your preferences at facebook.com/ads/preferences.

Google Ads Conversion Tracking & Remarketing

We use Google Ads to measure which ad clicks result in actions on our Website and to show Clockwrk ads to past Website visitors via the Google Display Network using the DoubleClick cookie and Google Ads tag. You can opt out at adssettings.google.com.

X (Twitter) Ads Conversion Tracking

We use the X Pixel to measure conversions from ads run on X and to build remarketing audiences of past Website visitors. Data is processed by X Corp. in the United States. You can manage preferences at twitter.com/settings/personalization.

LinkedIn Ads & Insight Tag

The LinkedIn Insight Tag connects data from LinkedIn ad campaigns with actions performed on this Website, enabling conversion tracking and retargeting of past visitors via the LinkedIn advertising network. You can opt out at LinkedIn opt-out settings.

Reddit Ads Conversion Tracking

We use the Reddit Pixel to measure conversions from ads run on Reddit and to build remarketing audiences. Data is processed by Reddit Inc. in the United States. You can manage preferences at reddit.com/settings/privacy.

Your Choices

Where required by law (e.g. for visitors from the EU and UK), advertising cookies and tracking pixels will only be activated after you provide your consent via our cookie consent mechanism. You may withdraw consent at any time by adjusting your cookie preferences or using the opt-out links above.

Visitors from all regions can also opt out of interest-based advertising through the Network Advertising Initiative or Your Online Choices (EU).

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure. These include:

  • HTTPS encryption across all pages of the Website

12. Additional Rights — EU & UK Users (GDPR)

Applies To: European Union & United Kingdom

If you are located in the EU or UK, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and, in the UK, the UK GDPR as retained in domestic law, grant you additional rights and protections.

Legal Bases for Processing

Under GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for each processing activity. The bases we rely on are set out in the table in Section 4 above (Performance of Contract, Legitimate Interest, Legal Obligation, and Consent). Where we rely on Legitimate Interest, you have the right to object and we will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests.

Additional Rights Under GDPR

  • Right to restriction of processing — request that we limit how we use your data while a dispute is resolved
  • Right to data portability — receive personal data you have provided to us in a structured, machine-readable format and have it transferred to another controller where technically feasible
  • Rights in relation to automated decision-making — we do not make any decisions about you based solely on automated processing that would produce legal or similarly significant effects

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe we have not handled your data in accordance with GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In the EU, find your national authority at edpb.europa.eu. In the UK, the supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Data Transfers from the EU/UK

When we transfer your data outside the EU/UK (e.g. to processors in the US), we do so using appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms approved under GDPR. Processors such as Google and Meta maintain their own EU data transfer compliance programmes.

13. Additional Rights — California Consumers (CCPA/CPRA)

Applies To: California, United States

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grants you specific rights over your personal information.

Categories of Personal Information Collected

We collect the following categories as defined under the CCPA:

  • Identifiers (name, email, IP address)
  • Commercial information (subscription details, payment history)
  • Internet or other network activity (browsing behaviour on our site, ad interaction data)
  • Professional or employment-related information (job applications)
  • Inferences drawn from the above (ad audience segments)

Sale or Sharing of Personal Information

Clockwrk does not sell your personal information for monetary consideration. However, our use of advertising pixels (Meta Pixel, Google Ads) may constitute "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CCPA. You have the right to opt out of this sharing.

Your CCPA Rights

  • Right to Know — request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months
  • Right to Delete — request deletion of personal information we have collected, subject to certain exceptions
  • Right to Correct — request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Right to Opt Out — opt out of the sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising by contacting us or adjusting your cookie preferences
  • Right to Non-Discrimination — we will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights

How to Exercise Your CCPA Rights

Submit requests to hello@clockwrk.io. We will respond within 45 days. We may request information to verify your identity. You may submit up to two requests in any 12-month period at no charge.

14. Additional Rights — Virginia Consumers (VCDPA)

Applies To: Virginia, United States

If you are a consumer residing in the Commonwealth of Virginia, the Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act ("VCDPA") grants you specific rights over your personal data. This section supplements the rest of this Privacy Policy and supersedes any conflicting information for Virginia residents.

Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We have collected the following categories of personal data: identifiers, commercial information, internet or other network activity information, geolocation data, employment-related information, and inferences drawn from other personal information. We do not collect sensitive data as defined under the VCDPA.

Sale of Your Personal Data

For our purposes, "sale" means any exchange of personal data for monetary consideration by us to a third party, as defined by the VCDPA. Our use of advertising pixels (Meta, Google Ads) may be considered a sale or targeted advertising under the VCDPA. You have the right to opt out.

Your Rights Under the VCDPA

  • Right to access — confirm whether we are processing your personal data and access such data
  • Right to correct — request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Right to delete — request deletion of personal data we have collected about you
  • Right to data portability — obtain a copy of your data in a portable, usable format
  • Right to opt out — opt out of the processing of your personal data for targeted advertising, sale, or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not deny goods or services or charge a different price based on your exercise of these rights

How to Exercise Your Rights

Submit requests to hello@clockwrk.io. We will respond within 45 days; we may extend by a further 45 days with notice. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. If we deny your request, you may appeal within 60 days and, if the appeal is denied, contact the Virginia Attorney General.

We do not charge a fee to respond, for up to two requests per year.
